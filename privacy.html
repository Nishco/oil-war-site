Oil War Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Oil War does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. The game does not require an account and has no online multiplayer, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, or user-generated content.

Information processed by Apple

Apple may process diagnostics or other information under your device, operating-system, and App Store settings. That processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy and controls; it is not collected by Oil War.

Children's privacy

Oil War does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. If a future version adds features that change this, this policy will be updated before release.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with the new effective date before that version is released.

Contact
